Your naming scheme works, with three small adjustments:

1. **Pad the number** (`_01`, `_02`, …). Without padding, `2026-09-23_10.md` sorts before `2026-09-23_2.md` in file listings.

If a day's first session were deleted, the second would be renumbered `_01` and overwrite the first transcript.
