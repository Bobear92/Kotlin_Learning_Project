## Wrap-up

That's all seven files. Here's what we covered:

- **Flyway migrations are a chain of changes**, never edited after they run. A new column means `V2__...` with `ALTER TABLE`. Editing V1 fails startup with a checksum mismatch.
- **Beans are singletons built at startup.** Constructor injection wires them bottom-up, missing wiring fails fast, and one object serving many threads is why beans must be **stateless**.
- **Layers each have one job:** the controller does HTTP, the service does rules and error meaning, the repository does SQL. Ordering comes from `ORDER BY`, not the controller.
- **Lambdas return their last expression.** `.query { }` runs once per row, and `.list()` executes the SQL and collects the returned items (like `.map`).
- **`data class`** generates `equals`, `toString` and `copy`. `==` compares values, not identity. `val` makes an item an immutable snapshot, and `copy` creates a new object.
- **Predict a test by tracing what it actually calls.** A test only breaks if the change sits on its path, and the STRICT JSON test is what guards the contract.
